04 / QUICK ANSWERS

Flight school basics.

What is SKYBOUND?

SKYBOUND is a browser-based aircraft design and flight game where you draw a plane, tune its settings, fly in 3D and race AI pilots.

Do I need to download anything?

No app installation is required. Open the game in a modern browser. A stable internet connection is needed to load the game and its 3D library.

Can I play without racing?

Yes. Choose Test flight for free flight, or Race to try the checkpoint course against AI pilots.

Can I save my aircraft?

Use Save in the hangar to store your current design in this browser, then use Load to bring it back on the same browser and device.

About SKYBOUND

SKYBOUND is a browser-based aircraft design and flight simulator game. Sketch an aircraft, experiment with its proportions and performance settings, then take it into a 3D flying environment. Practise in test flight or compete against AI pilots in checkpoint races.

Design your own aircraft

Use the drawing area to shape your aircraft outline. Try the mirror option for a symmetrical design, or start with an example aircraft. Adjust wing span, engine power, wing dihedral and paint hue to explore how your design changes.

Test flight and air racing

Choose Test flight to practise flying, or choose Race to navigate the checkpoint course against computer-controlled pilots. Watch your speed, altitude, throttle and angle of attack in the flight HUD.

Flight controls

  • W: pitch up · S: pitch down
  • A: turn left · D: turn right
  • Up / Down: adjust throttle
  • Space: boost
  • Shift: brake
  • C: change camera
  • R: reset aircraft
  • Esc: return to the workshop

Frequently asked questions

What is SKYBOUND?

SKYBOUND is an aircraft building and flying game. Draw a plane, tune its settings, fly in 3D and race AI pilots.

Can I design my own plane?

Yes. Draw an outline in the workshop and experiment with the available wing and engine settings before flying.

Can I play without racing?

Yes. Select Test flight for free flight, or choose Race for the checkpoint challenge.

What are the controls?

Use W to pitch up and S to pitch down; A turns left and D turns right. arrow keys adjust throttle, Space boosts, Shift brakes, C changes camera, R resets and Esc returns to the workshop.
